About Jeremy Olson

A researcher's background, a therapist's tradition

Jeremy Olson is based in Auckland, New Zealand. His work sits at the meeting point of two things: a research training in what makes for a good quality of life, and a practitioner's grounding in the gentle, solution-oriented therapeutic tradition — the same tradition that shapes the guided imagery on this site.

Postgraduate
Master of Social Sciences (Psychology), University of Waikato.
Research
Graduate quality-of-life research at Deakin University under Professor Robert Cummins, in the Personal Wellbeing Index tradition. This research was specifically into quality of life in spinal cord injury.
Clinical training
Diploma of Solution-Oriented Hypnosis with Dr Robert McNeilly, who studied in the tradition of Milton Erickson. Completed in 1998 at Ampersand, Melbourne.

Being straight with you

What Jeremy is, and isn't

A transparency note

Jeremy does not have Parkinson's

He also has no lived experience of spinal cord injury or stroke, the subjects of the companion sites. He isn't speaking as someone who has been through this condition, and he doesn't claim the particular authority that comes from that.

What he brings instead is a background in quality-of-life research and years of work in the Ericksonian, solution-oriented tradition — brought together here into a set of practices offered in good faith, and openly labelled throughout as a personal synthesis rather than a tested clinical protocol. The research and bibliography pages lay out exactly what the evidence does and doesn't support.
