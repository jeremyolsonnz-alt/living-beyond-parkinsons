Before you write

Your privacy. This site asks nothing of you — no sign-up, no email capture, no tracking for ads. If you email, your message is used only to reply to you, and for nothing else.

Please note

Jeremy can't offer medical advice, and this site isn't a place for urgent help. For anything about your symptoms, your medication, or your care, your GP, neurologist, or Parkinson's nurse is the right person to speak to. If you're in crisis or need urgent help, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line in your country.
